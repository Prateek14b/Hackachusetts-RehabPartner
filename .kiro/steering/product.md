# Product: Rehab.io

Rehab.io helps physical therapists (PTs) and patients stay coordinated between clinic visits.

- **PT side:** create a care plan (patient name/DOB/email, therapy type, exercises with sets, reps and a
  target range of motion set on an animated stick figure), print a PDF handout with a QR code, and
  review each patient's progress (personal-best ROM vs target, trends, session history, pain).
- **Patient side:** scan the QR, log in or register (name/DOB/email must match the plan), consent to
  camera use, then do a guided webcam session. MediaPipe tracks the body, counts reps and sets, and
  measures ROM. Only numbers are shared with the clinic, never video.

Principles:
- Accessible for older adults: large text and buttons, high contrast, plain language.
- Privacy first: video is processed in the browser and never uploaded.
- Describe results as monitoring, never diagnosis.
